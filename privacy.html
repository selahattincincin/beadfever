Beadfever

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short

Beadfever does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no accounts, and we run no servers of our own.

What stays on the device

Progress (finished works, retries, time played), necklaces made in the workshop and the parent settings are saved only on the device, in the app's own storage. They are deleted when the app is deleted, or from Parent area → Data. The only exception is the optional iCloud sync described below.

Optional iCloud sync (version 1.1 and later)

Sync is off by default. If a parent turns it on in the parent area, child profiles and progress are synchronised between the family's own devices through their own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage). This data is not sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see it. Turning the setting off stops the sync.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications created on the device. They are addressed to the parent, off by default and can only be turned on inside the parent area. No push notification service is used.

Purchases

The one-time Full Version purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no information that identifies you.

Spoken patterns

“Listen and thread” uses the speech voices built into iOS on the device. No recording is made and the microphone is never used.

Children's privacy

Beadfever is made for children under 6. Because no personal data is collected, no data about children is processed. Settings and links that leave the app are behind a parent gate.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: selahattincincin@gmail.com.
